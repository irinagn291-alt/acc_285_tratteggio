At cold launch the system may show the standard notification permission alert. There is no custom notifications usage-description string in the app’s property list. A camera usage string “This app does not use the camera.” is present in build settings, but the app never asks for camera access.

There is no login or accounts, no in-app purchase, no user-generated content shared with others, no account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Tratteggio is a quiet art quiz for people who want to learn public-domain paintings from a crop. You save works from the National Gallery of Art, frame a magnified shard, then patch the missing maker or title word from hanging chips. Restored pieces and misses stay on this device.

No credentials or demo account are needed. Cold launch shows a full-screen splash image, then either onboarding or the main quiz. If onboarding has not been finished, a three-page cover appears. Page 1 shows “Know it from a crop.” and “Finish the label from works you already saved, on this device.” with top-right “Skip” and bottom “Continue”. Page 2 shows “Frame, then patch.” and “Frame crops a saved work. One word is missing. Tap the chip that fills it.” with “Skip” and “Continue”. Page 3 shows “Restored stays here.” and “A miss stays reviewable. Saved keeps Restored pieces and misses.” with only “Continue”. “Skip” or finishing page 3 enters the main quiz titled “Patch the shard.” Tap “Explore”, then “Save” on a National Gallery of Art or local-shelf row (note “Saved.”). Close Explore to return to Quiz. Tap “Frame” when a shut work is available, then tap a word chip under “Tap a word.” to patch Maker or Title. Open “Saved” to browse Restored and Misses. Open “Settings” for Collection, Support (“Contact”, “Reset the crate”), Guide (“Frame then patch”), Undo, and “Re-run onboarding”.

Works, patches, and misses stay on this device. Support is available in Settings via “Contact”, which opens the support page.

Behaviour does not differ by region. The development region is English; numbers and dates follow the device locale. Appearance is forced light. Portrait only on iPhone and iPad. Minimum iOS 17.0.

No licence or regulated-industry paperwork applies. Collection credits point to the National Gallery of Art and open-access images; public-domain paintings come from the National Gallery of Art.

The app shows no health, medical, or product-health information. It is a personal log of Restored pieces and misses on this device and gives no recommendations.

The app does not scan barcodes or QR codes.
