Initial release of Tratteggio.

Save works from the National Gallery of Art into a personal crate, frame a magnified crop and patch the missing Maker or Title word from hanging chips. Restored pieces and misses stay on this device.

Includes Explore with search and a local shelf, Saved for Restored and Misses, Settings with Collection credits, Contact, Reset the crate, Re-run onboarding and the Frame then patch guide. Undo drops the newest patch or miss.
